# Miniproject 1 - The Two-Loan Comparison

**FRE 6103 Valuation for Financial Engineering (NYU Tandon)** | Raj Pawar (rsp9234)

A borrower needs $5,000 for two years with monthly payments and has two offers: Chase Bank at a 10% simple
annual rate with no fee, and Wells Fargo Bank at 7% with a 2% origination fee that is financed into the loan.
The workbook answers the five questions of the mini-project (textbook pp. 36-37) with live formulas.

| Sheet | Question |
|---|---|
| `1 Amortization` | (a), (b) monthly payment, amortization schedule and charts for both loans |
| `2 Payoff Analysis` | (c) payoff balance after each month against the present value of the remaining payments at Chase's rate |
| `3 Bank View` | (d) Wells Fargo's expected cash flows on 1,000 loans funded at 2%, with 6% annual defaults and 1% monthly prepayments |
| `Executive Summary` | (e) which loan the borrower should take, and why |

File: [`Miniproject1_TwoLoanComparison.xlsx`](Miniproject1_TwoLoanComparison.xlsx). Blue cells are given inputs,
black cells are formulas and green cells are links to another sheet.
