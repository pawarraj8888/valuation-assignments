Valuation Assignments

Mini-projects for Prof. Shimko's valuation course. Miniprojects 2 and 3 have their code, an Excel implementation, the write-up and a dashboard that shows the results in the browser. Miniproject 1 is an Excel workbook.

  • Miniproject 3, Part 1

    Simplified CDO Analysis

    1000 cases of correlated defaults on 10 bonds from fixed random numbers, the BIS debt model, and a Class A, Class B and equity waterfall. Pick any case or change the inputs and everything is recomputed.

    Raj Pawar and Michael Brick

    Open the dashboard
  • Miniproject 2

    Creating a ZCB Term Structure

    A continuously compounded zero-coupon curve fitted to every US Treasury note and bond quoted on September 4, 2026, with the discount rate for each payment date.

    Monalisa Maity and Raj Pawar

    Open the dashboard
  • Miniproject 1

    The Two-Loan Comparison

    Two $5,000 two-year loans compared in Excel: amortization schedules, early payoff, the bank's view on 1,000 loans with defaults and prepayments, and a recommendation.

    Raj Pawar
